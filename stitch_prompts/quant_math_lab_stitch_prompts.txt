10. GAUSSIAN PROCESSES — click on the plot to add observations, RBF kernel with sliders for lengthscale ℓ, signal variance σ_f and noise σ_n; posterior mean curve with shaded ±2σ uncertainty band; prior sample curves; kernel function plot.

11. PROBLEM SETS — auto-graded quiz: a math question, a text input for the answer, a CHECK button, green "correct" / red "wrong" feedback showing the right answer and full derivation, per-topic progress bars, a streak counter, and a "new problem" button that generates fresh random numbers.
